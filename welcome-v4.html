Quizzical — full update

Welcome to the new Quizzical

A lot has changed — here's the full picture of what's new and what's improved.

🤖 Quizzical is your daily quiz
  • Every day you get two AI-written quizzes — Morning (from 9am NZT) and Afternoon (from 3pm NZT)
  • Quizzes are played entirely inside the app — a step-by-step wizard with photos, instant feedback, and your score at the end
  • The old Stuff.co.nz quiz has been retired — no more embedded iframe or external site
  • Use the quiz dropdown in the header to pick any recent quiz, or tap Go to jump straight into today's
  • If today's quiz isn't ready yet, you'll see a message telling you when to check back
  • When you finish, post your score (and an optional comment) — it appears on the feed straight away
  • Questions cover NZ & Aussie current events, regional trivia, sports, geography, history, and general knowledge — with a photo for each question
🔍 Review past quizzes
  • Already done a quiz? It's still in the dropdown with your score (e.g. ✓ 12/15 (review))
  • Select it again to walk through every question, see what you picked, and what the correct answer was
  • Review mode is read-only — you can't change a score once it's posted
🏆 Rankings
  • A Rankings tab sits alongside the feed at the bottom of the screen
  • View leaderboards for Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, or All Time
  • Filter by Quizzical, Stuff (historical results from the old quiz), or All Types — Quizzical is the default
  • Your gold / silver / bronze feed badge (top 3 each week) is based on Quizzical results
  • See trend arrows comparing your average to the previous period
  • Track days active, streaks (🔥 badge for 3+ days), Most Improved, and Personal Bests
  • Tap any row to expand and see individual results
  • All Time hides inactive players (12+ months) in a collapsible Ghosts section
📋 Feed & posting
  • The feed loads more as you scroll — no fixed cutoff
  • Posting a comment or quiz score adds it to the top of the feed instantly — no full-page refresh
  • Profile photos in the feed are circular; top-ranked players get a coloured ring and rank number
✅ Smarter, more reliable quizzes
  • Questions are fact-checked against live sources before each quiz goes live — not just written and published
  • A final review pass checks all 15 questions together before the morning and afternoon quizzes are posted
  • Morning and afternoon quizzes should always appear — even on a difficult day, you'll get a full quiz rather than a blank slot
  • Sports questions are clearer — more AFL, NRL, and Super Rugby (colours, grounds, nicknames), and safer World Cup / host-year questions
  • Less repetition in a single quiz — the same famous treaty or event is much less likely to appear twice
  • Fewer debatable "world's largest / first ever / last country to…" questions that were often wrong
  • Geography questions are screened more carefully for dodgy premises
📎 Attachments
  • Attach photos and files to any post or comment (up to 10 at once)
  • Images show a preview thumbnail; tap to view full size
  • Any URL you type is automatically turned into a clickable link
💬 Comments
  • Comments show when they were posted
  • You can delete your own comments
🔔 Notifications
  • Email when someone comments on your post, digs your content, or when quiz results are posted (depending on your settings)
  • Control what you receive in your Profile settings
👤 Profile & account
  • Upload a profile photo with crop and rotate
  • Update your name, email, and password from the profile panel
  • Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the login page
  • New accounts log you in automatically — no extra step
★ What's New button
  • Tap ★ What's New in the header any time to read this again
  • This screen shows once automatically the first time you open the app after this update

If something in a quiz looks wrong, mention it in the group — it helps us keep improving.

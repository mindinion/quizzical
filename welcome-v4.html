Quiz quality update

What's new in Quizzical

We've spent a lot of time improving the AI that writes your daily quizzes. Here's what you'll notice.

✅ More accurate questions
  • Every quiz is now fact-checked against live sources before it goes live — not just written and published
  • A final review pass checks all 15 questions together before the morning and afternoon quizzes are posted
  • Sports questions are clearer and more reliable — especially around World Cups, host countries, and years (a common source of mistakes before)
  • Geography and general knowledge questions are screened more carefully for dodgy premises (e.g. a mountain range wrongly described as spanning two continents)
  • Fewer "world's largest / first ever / last country to…" style questions that sounded impressive but were often debatable or wrong
📅 Quizzes you can count on
  • Your morning (9am) and afternoon (3pm) quizzes should always appear — the generator keeps trying until it has a full quiz for you
  • On a difficult day you might occasionally get a question that's good enough rather than perfect, but you won't be left with a blank quiz slot anymore
  • Behind the scenes, the system accepts its best attempt rather than failing silently and leaving you with nothing
🎯 Less repetition in a single quiz
  • The same famous treaty or event (e.g. Treaty of Versailles, Magna Carta) is much less likely to appear twice in one quiz across different categories
  • Questions are checked against topics already used earlier in that quiz, so variety should feel better
🏉 Better sports trivia
  • Expect more AFL, NRL, and Super Rugby angles — team colours, nicknames, and home grounds
  • When a quiz does ask about a major tournament, the question is phrased and checked so the year matches the host country
  • Fewer confusing "which year did [team] win…" questions that were often marked with the wrong year
★ What's New button
  • Tap ★ What's New in the header any time to read this summary again
  • This screen shows once automatically the first time you open the app after this update

As always, if something looks wrong in a quiz, tell the group — it helps us keep improving.

Quizzical v4

Quizzical is now the main quiz

A few things have changed with how quizzing works.

🤖 Quizzical is the standard quiz
  • The AI-generated Quizzical Morning and Afternoon quizzes are now the only quizzes in the launcher
  • The old Stuff.co.nz quiz has been retired
  • Rankings, streaks and badges are now based on Quizzical results by default
🔍 Review past quizzes
  • Already completed today's quiz? Select it again from the dropdown to review your answers
  • You can see every question, your answer and the correct one — but you can't change your score
🏆 Rankings categories
  • The Rankings tab now has three categories: Quizzical, Stuff and All Types
  • Quizzical is the default — it's what your rank badge on the feed is based on
  • Switch to Stuff or All Types to see historical results from the old quiz

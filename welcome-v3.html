Quizzical v3

Welcome to the new Quizzical

A lot has changed. Here's what's new.

🏆 Rankings
  • A brand new Rankings tab sits alongside the feed at the bottom of the screen
  • View leaderboards by Weekly, Monthly, Yearly or All Time
  • See trend arrows comparing your average to the prior period
  • Track days active, streaks, Most Improved and Personal Bests
  • Tap any row to expand your individual results
📋 Quiz Launcher
  • Select today's quiz directly from the dropdown in the header
  • The quiz opens in an embedded window — no need to leave the app
  • Enter your score when done and it's logged instantly
  • Quizzes you've already completed show your score and are greyed out
📎 Attachments
  • Attach photos and files to any post or comment (up to 10 at once)
  • Images show a preview thumbnail; tap to view full size
  • Any URL you type is automatically turned into a clickable link
💬 Comments
  • Comments now show when they were posted
  • You can delete your own comments
🔔 Notifications
  • Email notifications when someone comments on your post or digs your content
  • Control what you receive in your Profile settings
👤 Profile
  • Upload a profile photo with crop and rotate
  • Update your name, email and password from the profile panel
  • Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the login page

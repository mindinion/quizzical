Quizzical — September 2026

What's new

Rankings now break down by topic, and the feed shows who’s strong in which categories.

🏆 Category rankings
  • On Rankings, switch between Overall and Categories — same weekly / monthly / yearly / all-time periods as before
  • Categories shows a leaderboard for each topic (History, Sports, Science & Technology, and the rest)
  • You need a minimum number of answers in a category for it to count — so short streaks don’t skew the table
🧩 Category emoji on feed posts
  • A small emoji beside someone’s name is their strongest category over the last 30 days
  • Tap the emoji for a breakdown: their top 3 categories, plus every category where they’re #1 in the group
  • Not everyone will have one yet — you need enough answers in a category before it appears
♦ 50/50 reminder
  • From question 10 onward, if you still have unused 50/50 lifelines, a gentle banner appears as a reminder
  • Tap the i icon on the banner if you want a quick explanation of how 50/50 works

Past scores, streaks and overall rankings are unchanged. Category stats build from your recent quiz answers. If anything looks wrong, mention it in the group.

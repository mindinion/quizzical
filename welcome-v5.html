What's new

We've improved how daily quizzes are written — you should notice more variety from quiz to quiz.

🔄 Less of the same, day after day
  • The quiz generator now remembers topics from recent quizzes, not just exact question wording — so the same subject is much less likely to keep turning up in a new form
  • That means fewer repeats of familiar angles like treaty questions, team-colour sports trivia, greenhouse-effect science questions, Petra, sugar gliders, and Rugby World Cup host-year questions crowding every other quiz
  • Within a single quiz, the same famous topic is still blocked twice — e.g. you won't get Versailles in History and again in general knowledge
  • Sports still includes AFL, NRL, and Super Rugby — but with more rotation between home grounds, nicknames, and venues, not the same colour question every time
  • History leans toward battles, inventions, and biographies when treaty questions have appeared recently

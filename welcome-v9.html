Quizzical — August 2026

What's new

A bug that could lock you out of a quiz has been fixed, and every past score has been repaired.

✓ Fixed: the quiz that wouldn’t let you in
  • Some quizzes showed as already done, with a score you didn’t recognise — usually one from a different day
  • Others jumped straight to the end-of-quiz screen, with no way back to the questions and no way to post your result
  • If a quiz was stuck like that for you, it should now open normally
📋 Why it happened
  • A posted score recorded when you posted it, but never which quiz it came from
  • So playing yesterday’s quiz this morning filed your score against today’s quiz instead — marking a quiz you’d never done as complete, and leaving the one you actually played unfinishable
  • Every result now remembers its own quiz, so the day you happen to play no longer matters
★ Catching up now works
  • You can play a missed quiz and today’s quiz on the same day and post both — previously the second one was silently refused
  • Results still appear in the feed when you played them, but are now labelled with the quiz’s own date
  • Tapping someone else’s result takes you to the quiz they actually did
🔍 Review answers, and clearer errors
  • The score screen has a Review answers button, so finishing a quiz without posting is no longer a dead end
  • If a result can’t be posted, the app now tells you why rather than just “something went wrong”

Every past Quizzical result has been reconnected to the quiz it came from. Your scores, streaks and rankings are unchanged — they simply point at the right quiz now. If anything still looks off, mention it in the group.

Quizzical — September 2026

What's new

The feed has been rebuilt around quizzes — results, comments and discussion are grouped by quiz instead of one long timeline of posts.

📋 Quiz cards on the feed
  • Each quiz is a card: title, how many have played, and when it was last active
  • Everyone’s scores sit underneath, ranked within that quiz — gold, silver and bronze numbers on profile photos for 1st, 2nd and 3rd
  • Tap Play (or your score) on the card to open the quiz; general group posts still appear as their own items
🏆 Weekly leaders at the top
  • The feed opens with the group’s top three for the week — photo, rank badge, and average score
  • Tap the category emoji beside a name for their 30-day category breakdown
  • The strip updates when someone posts a new result
🧩 Category emoji on each score
  • Beside each result is an emoji for that person’s best category in that quiz (not just their overall specialty)
  • Tap the emoji to see how they did in every category in that quiz
💬 Discuss a quiz
  • At the bottom of each quiz card: Discuss this quiz for comments about the quiz as a whole (separate from comments on individual scores)
  • Players, anyone who has already commented, and members with message notifications on will be notified
♦ 50/50 rankings
  • On Rankings, open the new 50/50 tab — same weekly / monthly / yearly / all-time periods as the rest
  • See group success rate, who has the best hit rate after using a lifeline, and how many were burned (used 50/50 and still got it wrong)
  • Tap a player to expand: which categories they use lifelines on, plus a bar chart of which question number they tend to spend them on — early burners on the left, hoarders on the right
🏆 Category rankings
  • Switch between Overall and Categories on the Rankings page
  • Categories shows a leaderboard for each topic (History, Sports, Science & Technology, and the rest)
  • You need a minimum number of answers in a category for it to count — so short streaks don’t skew the table
🧩 Category emoji on feed posts
  • On standalone group posts, a small emoji beside someone’s name is their strongest category over the last 30 days
  • Tap the emoji for a breakdown: their top 3 categories, plus every category where they’re #1 in the group
  • Not everyone will have one yet — you need enough answers in a category before it appears
♦ 50/50 reminder
  • From question 10 onward, if you still have unused 50/50 lifelines, a gentle banner appears as a reminder
  • Tap the i icon on the banner if you want a quick explanation of how 50/50 works

Past scores, streaks and overall rankings are unchanged. 50/50 stats only include lifelines used since that feature launched. If anything looks wrong, mention it in the group.

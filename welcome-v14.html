Quizzical — September 2026

What's new

The feed is built around quizzes, not a long timeline of score posts. Each quiz is a card; everyone’s results, comments and digs sit underneath it.

📋 Quiz cards
  • Each quiz shows its title, how many have played, and when it was last active (a new comment counts too)
  • Scores are listed in rank order — gold, silver and bronze numbers on profile photos for 1st, 2nd and 3rd
  • Comment and Dig work on individual scores the same as before; tap Play (or your score) on the card header to open the quiz
  • General group posts (not tied to a quiz) still appear on the feed as their own item
🏆 Weekly leaders
  • At the top of the feed: the group’s top three for the week — photo, rank badge, and average score
  • Updates when someone posts a new result
🧩 Category emojis
  • On a score within a quiz card, the emoji is that person’s best category in that quiz — tap it for the full breakdown
  • On the weekly leaders strip, the emoji is their strongest category over the last 30 days — tap for top categories and where they’re #1 in the group
  • Not everyone has one yet; you need enough answers in a category before it appears
💬 Discuss a quiz
  • At the bottom of each quiz card: Discuss this quiz for comments about the quiz as a whole, separate from comments on individual scores
  • Everyone who scored in that quiz, previous commenters, and members with message notifications on will be notified
📊 Rankings page
  • Overall and Categories — leaderboards for each topic (History, Sports, Science & Technology, and the rest). A minimum number of answers is required so short streaks don’t skew the table
  • 50/50 tab — group success rate, best hit rate after using a lifeline, and how many were burned (used 50/50 and still got it wrong). Tap a player to see which categories they use lifelines on and when in the quiz they tend to spend them
♦ 50/50 reminder
  • From question 10 onward, if you still have unused 50/50 lifelines, a gentle banner appears during the quiz
  • Tap the i icon if you want a quick explanation of how 50/50 works

Past scores, streaks and overall rankings are unchanged. If anything looks wrong, mention it in the group.

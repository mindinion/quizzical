Quizzical — July 2026

What's new

Daily quizzes are now built from a verified question bank plus fresh current-events questions — fewer wrong answers and less repetition.

📚 Smarter question mix
  • 11 questions come from a curated bank (Geography, History, General Knowledge) — verified trivia, not AI guesses
  • 4 questions are AI-written from national news headlines (2 NZ + 2 Australia)
  • Sports, NZ Trivia, and Australia Trivia categories are gone — replaced by this tighter mix
  • Bank questions won't repeat for 90 days, so you shouldn't keep seeing sugar gliders and the same battle every week
📰 Better current events
  • Headlines are ranked to favour national and world news over tiny local-town stories
  • Each quiz uses two different stories per region — no duplicate headline angles
🎯 Feed & answer stats
  • Tap a friend's quiz score bubble on the feed to jump straight into that quiz (play or review)
  • After you answer, see what % of players picked each option (once enough people have played)

Quizzical — September 2026

What's new

Rankings now cover topics and 50/50 lifelines, and the feed shows who’s strong in which categories.

♦ 50/50 rankings
  • On Rankings, open the new 50/50 tab — same weekly / monthly / yearly / all-time periods as the rest
  • See group success rate, who has the best hit rate after using a lifeline, and how many were burned (used 50/50 and still got it wrong)
  • Tap a player to expand: which categories they use lifelines on, plus a bar chart of which question number they tend to spend them on — early burners on the left, hoarders on the right
🏆 Category rankings
  • Switch between Overall and Categories on the Rankings page
  • Categories shows a leaderboard for each topic (History, Sports, Science & Technology, and the rest)
  • You need a minimum number of answers in a category for it to count — so short streaks don’t skew the table
🧩 Category emoji on feed posts
  • A small emoji beside someone’s name is their strongest category over the last 30 days
  • Tap the emoji for a breakdown: their top 3 categories, plus every category where they’re #1 in the group
  • Not everyone will have one yet — you need enough answers in a category before it appears
♦ 50/50 reminder
  • From question 10 onward, if you still have unused 50/50 lifelines, a gentle banner appears as a reminder
  • Tap the i icon on the banner if you want a quick explanation of how 50/50 works

Past scores, streaks and overall rankings are unchanged. 50/50 stats only include lifelines used since that feature launched. If anything looks wrong, mention it in the group.
